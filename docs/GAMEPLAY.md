# Gameplay

The crown waits in the old tower. Three acts lie between you and it, and what you do in the first decides what's possible in the last.

## Screens and controls

The game shows one thing at a time: read the story, press A, then choose.

| Button | Action |
|---|---|
| Up, down | Move the pointer between choices |
| A | Confirm, next |
| B | Next |
| START | Run stats page: build, seconds per word, seconds per event and bank switches measured on the device |
| SELECT (on the stats page) | New run |

The dark band shows where you are, what you carry and your remaining lives. The bottom bar shows the act and turn.

## How the story works

The story graph is in `reference/story.py`, and the ROM's tables are generated from it.

- **Act 1, forest to river:** you choose between the ruins (a lamp or a sword, not both) and the marsh, where a prisoner begs for help (free him, take his ring, or leave him).
- **Act 2, cave and shrine:** the cave needs the lamp or your freed friend. The key is inside, and the shrine has its own temptation.
- **Act 3, gate and tower:** the key, the sword, your friend or a dangerous climb get you through the gate. Then you face the guardian.

**Items change your options.** Choices appear only if you've earned them, and some outcomes depend on what you carry: sneaking past the guardian wearing the cursed ring wakes him.

**Omens.** The narration on arrival warns about the dangerous choice in that scene, such as "The old bridge groans." Read it and you can avoid being hurt.

**Wounds.** You have three lives. Dangerous choices cost one, and losing all three ends the run.

**Endings.** There are eight, and the ending screen counts how many you've found since power-on. Your count resets when the game is turned off, since there's no cartridge save.

`tests/test_story.py` checks that there are no dead ends, every scene and ending is reachable, no scene forces a single choice, and every run ends.

## What's generated and what's fixed

| Generated by the model on the Game Boy | Fixed in ROM |
|---|---|
| Every word of each story passage | Title, intro lines, place names, choice labels |
| Wording variations between playthroughs | Ending names and the screens around them |
| | Which event happened, always decided by the engine |
| | The capital letters added at sentence starts |

The sentences the model can produce were authored in `training/dataset.py`. It learned them, and it doesn't invent new plot.

## Art

All art and the font are original and generated by `tools/make_art.py`, with no third-party assets or Nintendo marks.
